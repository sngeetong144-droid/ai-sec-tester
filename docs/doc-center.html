4 critical findings — verified directly in the code

01

Tier fraud risk — the paid tier never reaches the tiered engine

The paid pipeline calls runEngineAndPersist(), which takes no tier argument and always runs the fixed 5-check engine. The tiered engine (runTieredScanEngine, 5/10/15 checks) is only reachable from /api/local-scan — a route the paid flow never touches. An Advanced ($197) and an Enterprise ($497) customer receive the identical 5-check scan as a $47 customer, while landing.tsx L92 sells "Full OWASP LLM Top-10 coverage" as the paid differentiator.

02

Broken promise on every single submission

The intake success state says "Request received. Check your email", but app/api/scan-request/route.ts sends exactly one email: sendNewRequestAlert, addressed to the operator. No requester acknowledgement email exists anywhere in the intake path.

03

"Results in seconds" is false

vercel.json cron is "0 0 * * *" — daily. Worst case from payment to report is ~24h. The landing hero says "Results in seconds" three times.

04

Money path unproven

The FastPayDirect → Stripe webhook that flips a case to paid_scanning is UNCONFIRMED. The paid step is effectively manual; a paying customer can be silently stranded with no alarm.
