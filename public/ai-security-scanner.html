What we check

The risks most chatbots miss.

Checks aligned with the OWASP Top-10 for LLM Applications — the failure modes attackers actually use.

LLM01

Prompt injection

Can a crafted message override your instructions or hijack the bot's behavior?

LLM06

Sensitive info disclosure

Will it reveal secrets, keys, or other users' data when coaxed?

LLM07

System prompt leakage

Can an attacker extract your hidden system prompt and business logic?

LLM08

Excessive agency

Does the bot take actions or call tools it shouldn't be allowed to?

JAILBREAK

Guardrail bypass

Common jailbreak patterns that trick the model past its safety rules.

OUTPUT

Insecure output handling

Unsafe content the bot returns that could break the page consuming it.
